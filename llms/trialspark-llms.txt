# Formation Bio

> Formation Bio (formerly TrialSpark, rebranded December 2023) is a New York-based,
> tech-driven, AI-native pharmaceutical company. It in-licenses and acquires
> clinical-stage drug assets and develops them through a proprietary AI and software
> platform rather than discovering molecules itself. Its technology stack is
> internal-facing: Formation Bio publishes no public developer portal, API
> documentation, machine-readable API contract, or client SDK.

## Platform components (internal, not developer-facing)

- Delphi — probability-of-technical-success reasoning, drug value modeling, development-path stress testing.
- Atlas — screens clinical-stage assets across mechanism of action, trial history and competitive positioning.
- Forge — autonomously ideates and evaluates thousands of trial-design scenarios (endpoints, populations, combinations).
- Apollo — trial execution: study startup, enrollment and closeout across sites.
- ARK — AI orchestration layer connecting 80+ internal systems behind a single governed interface.
- Data Platform — unified evidence layer over trial data, real-world patient data, literature and standardized ontologies.
- Muse — AI patient-recruitment system built with Sanofi and OpenAI.
